A brand-new look for the **MSSB Clone Maker**: your clone in 3D, front and centre, with
everything you need around it.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows | **MSSB-Clone-Maker.exe**: download and double-click, nothing to install. |
| Mac | **MSSB-Clone-Maker-Mac.zip**: unzip, then see [first launch on Mac](https://github.com/Cjericho4/mssb-modding/blob/main/tools/clonemaker/README.md#1-get-the-app). |

Already using the Clone Maker? Download the new file and replace the old one. Games you made
earlier keep working.

## What's new

- **See your clone in 3D.** The middle of the window shows the selected clone as it looks in the
  game, wearing its new colours, with its hands and glove, in the fielding stance. Drag it to turn
  it round, or use **Front**, **Side**, **Back**, the turn arrows and slow spin under it.
- **All the textures, full size.** **Textures** (top right of the stage) shows every texture of the
  character before and after, as big as the window allows.
- **A new layout.** A slim dock on the left shows a badge for each character you clone and a dot for
  each clone; open it for names, a ✓ or ⚠ per clone, and a **Checks** card that says what still
  needs fixing (for example two clones on the same slot). On the right, one tab has everything about
  the clone (slot, colour, which colours change, shading, own textures) and the other everything
  about the character it copies (**These clones copy**, exports, animations).
- **Starts simpler.** The app asks for your game when it opens, starts with one clone instead of
  four, and a short **tour** shows you around the first time. **Take the tour** in Advanced options
  shows it again.
- **Themes.** **Night** (the new default), **Honey**, **Ballpark**, **Light** and **Strawberry milk**,
  under Advanced options.
- **Right-click menus.** Right-click a clone, a character or the model for shortcuts. The old
  right-click **Reload**, which threw your setup away, is gone: **Start over…** asks first, and so do
  F5 and Ctrl+R / Cmd+R.
- **Yoshi's glove matches.** The bit of Yoshi's green hand on his glove now changes colour with the
  rest of him (likewise for other colours that are nothing like the glove's leather).
- **Export 3D Model includes the hands and glove**, attached to the wrists.
- **Custom colours on Mac.** The rainbow swatch opens the colour picker again (it may need a
  double-click).

## Known issues

- **Windows Smart App Control** may block the app because it isn't signed yet; the
  [troubleshooting section](https://github.com/Cjericho4/mssb-modding/blob/main/tools/clonemaker/README.md#troubleshooting)
  shows what to try.
- On a Mac the rainbow (custom colour) swatch may need a double-click.
- Borrowing another character's animations isn't well tested yet: it can look glitchy in a few moves
  and may change hitboxes.
- On the select screen the big 3D character shows the cloned character in its own colours, not the
  clone's colour.
- Some recolours have rough spots, especially on Donkey Kong and Peach. For Peach, tick both **Pink (dress)** and **Dark pink (dress)** to recolour the whole dress.
- Not every character has been played as a clone in Rio yet (Mario, Peach, King Boo and DK have).
- The Mac app isn't signed, so macOS blocks the first launch; the user guide shows how to open it.
- Don't use a patched game for Rio's ranked play.
