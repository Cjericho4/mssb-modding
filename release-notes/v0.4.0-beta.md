A cleaner, easier-to-read **MSSB Clone Maker** window. Everything works the same way; it's just
clearer what to do next.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows | **MSSB-Clone-Maker.exe**: download and double-click, nothing to install. |
| Mac | **MSSB-Clone-Maker-Mac.zip**: unzip, then see [first launch on Mac](https://github.com/Cjericho4/mssb-modding/blob/main/tools/clonemaker/README.md#1-get-the-app). |

Already using the Clone Maker? Download the new file and replace the old one. Games you made
earlier keep working.

## What's new

- **A tidier window.** More even spacing, clearer headings and step numbers, and one big button
  that stands out: **Create patched game**. **Export Textures**, **Export 3D Model**,
  **Add another clone** and **Advanced options** are quieter, so they don't compete with it.
- **Clearer before you start.** Until you choose your game, **Choose your game** is highlighted
  and the Clones step tells you what comes next, instead of showing empty columns.
- **Fits small windows.** At the smallest window size the clone rows now fit without scrolling
  sideways.
- **Better dialogs.** Questions and messages now dim the window behind them and stand out from it.
- **Easier with a keyboard.** Every button, box and slider shows a clear outline when you Tab to
  it, Tab stays inside an open dialog, and all the text is easier to read against the background.

## Known issues

- On the select screen the big 3D character shows the cloned character in its own colours, not the
  clone's colour.
- Animations marked **"Some bones differ"** can look glitchy in a few moves.
- Some recolours have rough spots, especially on Donkey Kong and Peach. For Peach, tick both **Pink (dress)** and **Dark pink (dress)** to recolour the whole dress.
- Not every character has been played as a clone in Rio yet (Mario, Peach, King Boo and DK have).
- The Mac app isn't signed, so macOS blocks the first launch; the user guide shows how to open it.
- Don't use a patched game for Rio's ranked play.
